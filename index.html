Flxpped Co

Where Fashion Meets Entrepreneurship.

For as long as I can remember I have loved fashion. I am grateful to have learned all of my skills from my grandmother, who put a needle and thread into my hands as soon as I could hold them. From there, I have been able to combine my love for fashion with a passion for sustainability and my never-ending need to engineer into my project, Flxpped Co, where I create upcycled items from second-hand clothing.

Through this I have learned how to manipulate materials in all forms, apply my engineering skills to convert 2D shapes into 3D items, and create really unique designs to wear.
